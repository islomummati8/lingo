Learning English: An Essential Skill in Today's World

Fida Abo Hasson

Published on Lingo Insights • 5 min read

English is widely recognized as the global language of communication, business, travel, and modern technology. Whether you are browsing the internet, engaging in international trade, or pursuing higher education, knowing English opens doors to endless opportunities.

Why Practice Every Day?

Consistency is the absolute key to mastering any language. Spending even 15 to 20 minutes a day listening, speaking, or reading in English builds neural pathways that make retention much easier. At Lingo, our expert instructors focus on practical application rather than just memorizing grammar rules.

Top 3 Tips from Lingo Instructors:

  • Immerse yourself: Change your phone, laptop, and social media settings to the target language.
  • Don't fear mistakes: Making mistakes means you are actively trying and learning.
  • Speak from day one: Practice with native or expert tutors via interactive sessions.
