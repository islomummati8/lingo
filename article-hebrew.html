Learning Hebrew: Why Arabic Speakers Have a Strong Advantage

Because Hebrew and Arabic share deep Semitic roots, grammar patterns and vocabulary share striking similarities.

Semitic languages possess a root-based morphological structure. For Arabic speakers learning Hebrew, recognizing root stems (shorashim) provides an incredible shortcut to vocabulary retention.
